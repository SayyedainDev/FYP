## 🎯 What Works Now

### ✅ Complete Workflow
```
User Login
    ↓
Add Patient (appears immediately)
    ↓
Create Case (select patient from dropdown)
    ↓
Upload X-ray Images (multi-select)
    ↓
Analyze Case (3-second simulation)
    ↓
View AI Results (cavity detection, confidence %)
    ↓
Case Saved (to Firestore + subcollection)
    ↓
View in History (real-time update)
    ↓
Dashboard Updates (patient/case counts)
```
